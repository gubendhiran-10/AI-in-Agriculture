System Overview & Architecture

This AI-Based Emergency Resource Allocation System bridges field IoT sensor telemetry with Machine Learning disaster classification and priority linear optimization. By replacing slow manual damage surveys with real-time bio-climatic analytics, the model automatically detects Droughts, Floods, Pest Outbreaks, and Crop Fires, prioritizes regions through weighted severity scoring, and allocates vital resources (Water, Personnel, Heavy Machinery, Funds) to the most vulnerable farming communities.

System Architecture & Tech Stack

🧠

AI & ML Engine

Multi-factor decision models evaluate soil moisture, heat indices, precipitation accumulation, and wind gusts to classify agricultural emergencies.

🗄️

Dual Database Layer

Resilient storage with active MongoDB document collections and automatic zero-latency fallback to SQLite (agriculture.db) for maximum availability.

📐

Linear Optimization

Priority-weighted allocation distributes finite relief supplies (water tankers, rescue teams, machinery, and funds) according to crisis urgency.

🗺️

Leaflet.js Geospatial GIS

Interactive map visualization displaying geographical coordinates, radius sizing based on severity scores, and real-time sensor popups.

🎙️

Speech & AI Assistant

Voice synthesis and speech recognition powered by Web Speech API, with context-aware intelligence on live disaster state.

⚡

Action Operations Center

Lifecycle management of emergency incidents: Automated Detection → Resource Mobilization (Dispatched) → Disaster Containment (Resolved).

Project Information & Working Model Summary

This project was developed by Dhanush (Department of Computer Science & Engineering) to demonstrate the transformative impact of AI, IoT, and Operations Research on agricultural disaster management. By continuously processing telemetry and applying mathematical optimization, relief efforts can reach disaster-stricken farming belts up to 67% faster than legacy bureaucratic procedures.
